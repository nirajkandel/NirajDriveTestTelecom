# Privacy Policy — Niraj Drive Test

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Niraj Drive Test ("the app") is a mobile-network measurement tool. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## What the app accesses
- **Precise location (GPS)** — to record where each measurement was taken and draw the drive route on the map. Android also requires location permission for an app to read cell-tower information.
- **Phone state** — to read the network type, operator name and the list of SIM cards so you can choose which SIM to measure.
- **Cell information** — serving and neighbour cell identifiers and signal values (MCC, MNC, TAC/LAC, Cell ID, PCI, ARFCN, RSRP, RSRQ, SINR and similar).

While a drive test is running, the app keeps collecting location and cell data in the background, with a permanent notification, even if the screen is off. Collection stops as soon as you press STOP.

## What happens to the data
- All measurements are stored **only on your device**, as CSV files inside the app's own storage.
- The app has **no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server**. It does not upload your measurements anywhere.
- Data leaves your device only when **you** choose to share or save a CSV file (for example to Gmail, Google Drive or WhatsApp).
- Map tiles are downloaded from OpenStreetMap. As with any web request, the tile server sees your IP address and the map area being viewed. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

## Deleting data
Delete individual logs from the LOGS tab, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
